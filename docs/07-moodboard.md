# Sham Dental — Visual Moodboard Direction

> The feeling: walking into a serene, light-filled Aman Resort that happens to
> have the precision of an Apple lab. Clinical confidence without coldness.

## 1. Mood in three words
**Serene · Precise · Radiant.**

## 2. Reference DNA
| Source | What we borrow |
|--------|----------------|
| **Apple.com** | Generous negative space, oversized confident type, product-as-hero, restrained motion |
| **Tesla.com** | Full-bleed cinematic hero, minimal nav, scroll-told story |
| **Aesop.com** | Editorial calm, material honesty, muted sophistication |
| **Aman Resorts** | Stillness, light, luxury-as-calm, human warmth |
| **Dubai luxury real estate** | Aspiration, gloss, "this is for me" desire, gold-grade finish |
| **High-end cosmetic/medical** | Clean clinical trust, science made beautiful |

## 3. Color story
Primary brand blue + teal carry **trust + vitality/health**; deep ink provides
cinematic contrast; soft near-white surfaces keep it airy and premium.

```
Sham Blue   #317EBB   trust, professionalism, calm
Sham Teal   #1D938D   health, freshness, vitality
Gradient    blue → teal   energy, modernity, the "signature"
Ink         #0C1418   cinematic depth, focus
Background  #F8FAFB   light, clinical cleanliness, space
Surface     #FFFFFF   purity, premium product feel
Text        #1B1F23 / #5E6B75   quiet authority
```
**Ratio:** ~70% light/neutral, ~20% ink (dramatic sections), ~10% brand
gradient (accents & CTAs). Gradient is precious — never wallpaper.

## 4. Typography mood
- **Cormorant Garamond** (display, italic accents): elegance, the human/beauty
  story, the "smile". High-contrast serif = couture, not clinic.
- **Manrope** (UI/body): modern, geometric, trustworthy, highly legible.
- **Tajawal** (Arabic): clean, contemporary, harmonizes with Manrope; preserves
  luxury in RTL.
- **Pairing logic:** emotional headlines in serif, factual/credibility content
  in sans. Big size jumps; tight display leading; airy body.

## 5. Photography & video direction
**Do**
- Cinematic, shallow depth of field, soft natural/golden light.
- Authentic, diverse, confident smiles — real emotion, candid joy.
- Macro detail of technology (3D scanner glow, instruments) shot like product.
- Architectural shots of calm, light-filled, design-led clinic interiors.
- Slow-motion smile/laughter footage for hero; subtle, never frantic.
- Consistent warm-cool grade that lets blue/teal accents sing.

**Don't**
- Generic stock "thumbs-up dentist", exaggerated white teeth, fake smiles.
- Clinical gore, harsh fluorescent medical-blue, cluttered rooms.
- Busy backgrounds; anything that reads "template".

## 6. Texture, light & finish
- **Glassmorphism** for floating UI (blur, thin light borders) — luxury tech.
- **Soft radial mesh lighting** & gradient glows in dark sections — cinematic.
- **Subtle grain** over hero — filmic, premium, anti-flat.
- **Generous radii** (18–40px) — friendly, modern, expensive.
- **Soft, long shadows** — depth without heaviness.
- Micro-sheen on primary buttons — jewelry-like polish.

## 7. Iconography & graphic language
- Thin (1.6px) line icons, rounded joins — medical-precise yet gentle.
- The mark: a stylized tooth/leaf hybrid (health + nature) in the gradient.
- Numbered tags & eyebrows in tracked uppercase sans — editorial structure.
- Data/credibility shown as elegant counters, not clip-art badges.

## 8. Layout & space
- Wide container (1320), large gutters, lots of breathing room.
- Asymmetry with intent (feature service card, split hero, story two-column).
- Strong vertical rhythm; each section a distinct "scene" (light ↔ dark cadence).

## 9. Motion mood
Weighted, slow, decelerating (ease-lux). Reveals like a curtain lifting, not a
bounce. Looping ambient motion (mesh drift, Ken-Burns, FAB pulse) stays
barely-there. **Calm confidence in motion** — see `03-animation-spec.md`.

## 10. Emotional verdict to engineer
Every visual choice should make a first-time visitor think:
> _"These people are precise, calm, modern, and clearly the best. I'd trust them
> with my smile — and my family's."_
